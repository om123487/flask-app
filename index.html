🛡️ Phishing Prevention Tips

Follow these basic security practices to stay safer online.

01

Check the URL

Carefully inspect the website address before entering personal information.

02

Look for HTTPS

Check whether the website uses HTTPS, but remember that HTTPS alone does not guarantee a website is safe.

03

Avoid Suspicious Links

Don't blindly click links received through unknown emails, messages, or social media accounts.

04

Never Share Passwords

Never enter sensitive information on websites you do not trust.
